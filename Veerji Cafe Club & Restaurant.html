Our story

A warm table in the heart of Satna, built for family, friends and good music.

✦

More than a restaurant

Veerji Cafe Club & Restaurant is a lively pure vegetarian restaurant on the first floor of R.S Tower at Circuit House Square. It is a place where friends meet over coffee, families come for long dinners and evenings end with live music.

We are also a popular venue for celebrations. Birthdays, kitty parties, engagements and office get-togethers all find a home here, with food, music and seating taken care of.
